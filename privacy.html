Who We Are

Reborn Tech Global is a top African leading tech company based in Nigeria. This policy explains how we handle the personal information you give us through this website. Our contact details are in the footer of this page.

What We Collect

We collect the details you give us, whether in our forms or when you message us on WhatsApp or by email:

  • Your name.
  • Your WhatsApp number.
  • Your email address.
  • The details or message you write.
  • For partners, a company name, website and CAC number, or a link to your portfolio or profile.

When you visit, our hosting provider and the services that deliver our video and images may record technical details such as your IP address and device type. This website saves your Light or Dark choice on your device. We do not use advertising cookies.

How Our Forms Work

  • Request a Service: the form opens WhatsApp with your message ready. This website does not store it. We receive your message only when you send it on WhatsApp.
  • Partner With Us: your answers are sent to a Google Sheet that we manage.

Why We Use Your Information

We use your details to:

  • Reply to your request or message.
  • Carry out the service you ask for.
  • Review partnership requests.
  • Keep records of our work.
  • Protect against fraud and misuse.

Nigerian data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), applies to how we handle your information. We rely on your consent and on the steps you ask us to take before we agree to work together.

Who Sees Your Information

Only us and the tools we use to run the business:

  • Google (Sheets and Apps Script) for partner requests.
  • WhatsApp and our email service for messages.
  • Cloudinary to deliver our videos.

These providers handle information under their own terms. We do not sell your data. We may share information if the law requires us to.

Links to Other Websites

Our pages link to other websites, such as our social media pages and projects we have built. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and we do not control them.

How Long We Keep It

We keep your details only as long as we need them for the reasons above, and for 1 month after our last contact. After that we delete them.

We may keep some records longer if the law requires it. [Add any longer period for exchange records, such as identity checks, with legal advice]

Your Rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete your information.
  • Stop using your information, if you gave us consent and now take it back.

Use the contact details in the footer. We will reply within 72 hours. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information.

No website or online service is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version. If we make a big change, we will say so on this page.
